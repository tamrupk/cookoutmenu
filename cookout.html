Cookout Burgers

Burgers are a central part of the Cookout menu. Customers can choose a simpler hamburger or try specialty styles with additional toppings and sauces. Depending on the restaurant, options may include a regular burger, a Big Double Burger, Cookout Style, Cheddar Style, and Out West Style.

  • Regular Hamburger: A straightforward choice for customers who prefer a classic burger.
  • Big Double Burger: A larger option with two beef patties.
  • Cookout Style: Commonly associated with toppings such as chili, slaw, mustard, and onion.
  • Cheddar Style: A richer combination that may include cheddar cheese, bacon, grilled onion, and mayonnaise.
  • Out West Style: A barbecue-inspired option with toppings such as barbecue sauce, bacon, and onion.

Availability and ingredients may differ by location. If you have an allergy or dietary requirement, ask restaurant staff to confirm ingredients and preparation practices.

Cookout Chicken, Hot Dogs, Barbecue, and Quesadillas
